# Locked reconstruction protocol

This independent reconstruction replaces unverified legacy numerical claims.
The archived code has been recovered for audit, but its stored CSV is not raw run evidence.

- Data: three synthetic demand regimes (seasonal, intermittent, correlated), each 480 cycles and 12 terminal demand streams; UCI Online Retail, 374 daily cycles, 12 SKUs selected using training-period volume only. Real SKU traces are scaled into interchangeable synthetic demand units (training mean 8); no claim of an observed material network is made.
- Graph: 16 nodes, three echelons (one root, three intermediate nodes, twelve terminals), 15 material edges and 15 reverse information edges. Same topology across cases; cases are not four independent real-world datasets.
- Fixed data seeds in the executed separate-case commands: 101 for each synthetic demand generator and 1001 for each simulator. Common random numbers are used across cases; the regimes are not independent replications. Training/model seeds: 11,23,37,53,71. Fixed chronological 60/20/20 split, 20-cycle warmup, horizon two cycles, six-cycle history. All model and feature selection uses training/validation only.
- Inventory: proportional allocation; order-up-to policy; nominal transit two cycles, one-cycle periodic review, base stock mean*(L+1)+std*sqrt(L+1). Per-node shipment capacity 1.6*calibrated mean; independent 4% disruption probability each cycle reduces capacity to one quarter. Conservation asserted each cycle. External emergency purchases and root supply counted separately.
- Shift evaluation without retraining: unchanged, transit lead increases 2 to 3 at test boundary, or order-up-to targets multiplied by 0.65 at test boundary. These are synthetic stress interventions.
- Methods: persistence; local Gaussian balance heuristic (not an optimal echelon-base-stock predictor); XGBoost; shared-weight LSTM; homogeneous mean aggregation GNN; relational mean aggregation GNN; homogeneous mean-GNN plus GRU; homogeneous attention; relational attention plus GRU; HetST relation-aware snapshot attention.
- Neural budget: 40 max epochs per candidate, 8-epoch patience, batch 64, AdamW weight decay 1e-4, gradient norm clipped at 5, LR candidates 0.002/0.0006, fixed width16, heads2, layers3, dropout0.1. No claim of equal parameter counts or equal wall-clock budgets. XGBoost depth3/6, 120 trees, LR0.05. All neural models use training class-weighted BCE plus positive-only MSE; validation selects minimum same objective.
- Metrics: macro F1, AUROC, average precision, Brier score; normalized conditional positive RMSE; mixture p*q RMSE over all nodes. Fixed classification threshold0.5. The weighted BCE can miscalibrate probabilities; Brier and closed-loop outcomes are reported without claiming calibrated risk.
- Event warnings: only non-backordered nodes, rising alert episodes, earliest unmatched onset within two cycles. Exclude right-censored warnings and event onsets without a full lookback; report conditional lead time alongside event recall, precision and false-alert frequency. Lead time cannot exceed the matching window.
- Decision policy: at each test decision t, allocate external emergency supply arriving t+2; total cap 15% of calibrated terminal demand per cycle; sort terminal nodes by p, require p>=0.5, cap amount at conditional predicted shortage. Fixed policy, no test cost tuning; compare no intervention, uniform, persistence and other predictors. Costs: holding0.1/unit-cycle (all nodes), customer backlog5/unit-cycle (terminals), emergency purchase2/unit. A model's realized policy path feeds back into subsequent model states. Common demand/disruption realizations. Finite test-horizon accounting, not infinite-horizon optimal control.
- Ablations: no temporal encoding, no edge features, shared relation weights (HomGAT), one head, no self term, classification-only, regression-only. RMSE of a classification-only untrained head must not be reported.
- No claim of observed FMCG validation, exact recovery of original results, superiority over all published methods, or guaranteed Q1 readiness.
